# The Doorway Reset

**Draft 1** · Free opt-in audio for Bold Heart Coaching · Target length about 10 minutes

## Recording notes

- Read slowly, at roughly half your normal talking speed. The script runs about 950 words, so the pauses fill out the 10 minutes.
- `[pause]` means 3 to 4 seconds. `[long pause]` means 8 to 10 seconds.
- Lower your voice a little on the words in *italics*. Those are the embedded suggestions.
- Change any line that doesn't sound like you. A line you would really say beats a perfect line.
- Leave 5 seconds of silence at the start and the end.

---

## Script

### Opening (about 1 minute)

Hey. This is Chuck, from Bold Heart Coaching.

This is the Doorway Reset. It's for that moment right before you walk in the door at home, when the day is still riding on your shoulders.

If you're in your car, make sure you're parked with the engine off. Don't listen to this while driving. If you're somewhere else, a bench or a quiet corner works just as well.

You don't have to do this right. You don't even have to believe it will work. You only have to listen. [pause]

### Settling (about 2 minutes)

Go ahead and let your hands rest wherever they want to rest. Your lap is fine. The steering wheel is fine. [pause]

And you can let your eyes close now, or you can keep them open and let them settle on one spot. Some people find their eyes want to close on their own after a while. [pause]

Take a breath in through your nose. Fill up a little more than usual. [pause]

And let it go out slowly through your mouth, as if you're fogging a window. [pause]

One more like that. In. [pause] And out, slower this time. [long pause]

Now let your breathing go back to doing what it does. You don't need to manage it. It has kept you alive all day without any help from you. [long pause]

### Setting the day down (about 3 minutes)

You've been carrying things all day. Some of them you chose. A lot of them you didn't. [pause]

The meeting that went sideways. The email you're still answering in your head. The guy who cut you off. The thing someone said that you didn't get to answer. [pause]

You don't have to solve any of it right now. You only have to notice it's there. [pause]

Notice where the day is sitting in your body. For a lot of men it's the jaw. For some it's the shoulders, or a tight spot behind the ribs. [pause] Wherever it is for you, just notice it. You don't have to fix it. [long pause]

And as you notice it, you might find it starts to loosen on its own. Maybe a little. Maybe more than you'd expect. [pause]

Your jaw can *let go* a quarter of an inch. [pause] Your shoulders can drop toward your hips. [pause] Your hands can get a little heavier. [long pause]

Now picture a bag sitting next to you. Any kind of bag. A gym bag, a briefcase, an old duffel. [pause]

One at a time, you can put the day into it. The meeting goes in the bag. [pause] The email goes in the bag. [pause] The traffic goes in. [pause] Whatever else is still buzzing, it goes in the bag too. [long pause]

The bag isn't going anywhere. It will be right here tomorrow if you want it. You're just not bringing it inside tonight. [pause]

Zip it closed. [pause] And *leave it in the car*. [long pause]

### The doorway (about 2 minutes)

Now think about the door you're about to walk through. Your front door, or the door from the garage. You know exactly what it looks like. [pause]

A doorway is a strange thing. On one side you're the guy who handles problems all day. On the other side, there are people who don't need you to handle anything. They just want *you*. [pause]

Your body already knows how to switch. You do it every time you laugh at something without thinking. You do it every time you see your kid or your dog and your face changes before you decide to change it. [pause]

So when your hand touches that door handle tonight, let that be the signal. [pause]

Hand on the handle. [pause] One slow breath out. [pause] And you *walk in as the man you want to be in there*. [long pause]

You might notice you speak a little slower. You might notice you look at whoever is there before you look at your phone. You might notice that the small stuff stays small. [pause]

And if something does get to you tonight, and sometimes something will, you can come back to that breath. One slow breath out. The door is always there. [long pause]

### Rehearsal (about 1 minute)

Let's walk through it once, right now, while you're sitting here. [pause]

See yourself getting out of the car. [pause] Walking to the door. [pause] The bag stays behind. [pause]

Hand on the handle. [pause] Slow breath out. [pause]

Open the door. [pause] And walk in. [long pause]

Notice what it feels like to arrive like that. [long pause]

### Coming back (about 1 minute)

In a moment I'm going to count from one to five. With each number you'll come back a little more, clear and awake, with the calm still with you. [pause]

One. Feel the seat under you. [pause]

Two. Wiggle your fingers. [pause]

Three. Take a fuller breath. [pause]

Four. Let your eyes open, if they closed. [pause]

Five. Wide awake. Clear-headed. Ready. [pause]

The bag stays in the car. Hand on the handle, one breath out, and go on in.

They're glad you're home. [pause] Go see them.

## Things to check before recording

- **Name:** the script opens with your public pen name. Confirm that's the name you want on the audio.
- **Safety:** the "parked with the engine off" line protects you and the listener. Keep it.
- **No claims:** the script makes no promises about anger, health or therapy outcomes. Keep it that way in the opt-in page copy too.
- **Length:** time your first read-through. If it runs under 9 minutes, lengthen the `[long pause]` marks before adding words.
