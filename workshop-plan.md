- Who I serve: men who hold it in all day, then snap at the people they care about
- What I sell today (if anything): nothing yet. Past 1:1 sessions sold for $100 to $300.
- Current audience size (email list, social, clients): no list, no followers. 10 to 20 past clients, mostly peers and women.
- Past launches or workshops: none launched. Earlier ideas stalled for lack of focus and time.

### Constraints and preferences

- Time: 5 to 10 hours a week, marketing included
- Goal: first paying clients within 90 days. Long-term goal is $4,000+/month net.
- Budget: about $100/month for Meta ads (approved), plus existing tools
- Energizing work: deep 1:1 sessions, teaching groups live, recording audio, building systems with AI
- Avoid: sales and discovery calls. A 15-minute fit call for safety screening is acceptable.
- Privacy: nothing public reveals my legal name, so first clients come from strangers online instead of my personal network
- Risk to manage: my free hours drift into building tools. A build freeze starts once the plan is agreed.

## Focus plan (90 days)

**The funnel:** faceless videos and Meta ads → Doorway Reset audio (opt-in) → 5 welcome emails → free monthly Zoom workshop → Reset Session → 5-session program

### Offer ladder

| Step | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Doorway Reset | Free | 10-minute audio to play before walking in the door at home |
| Workshop | Free | 60-minute live Zoom, monthly |
| Reset Session | $300 or $500 | One deep session, two options: $300 for up to 90 minutes, or $500 for up to 3 hours. The 90-day goal. |
| 5-session program | $2,000, later up to $5,000 | Offered only to men who had a breakthrough in the Reset Session |

### Paused for 90 days

- Skool community, until 10+ men have worked with me. Then open it as a founding group.
- YouTube and TikTok. Facebook and Instagram only.
- Weekly 1,200-word blog. Workshop recordings become posts instead.
- Paid-workshop machinery (sales page, order bump, upsells) until workshop #3 or later
- UplandMade, practitioner training for Rich's audience, new skills or tools

### Weekly time

| Activity | Time |
|---|---|
| Record 4 voiceovers | 1 hour |
| Approve what Claude drafts | 1 hour |
| Workshop prep and delivery, averaged | about 1 hour |
| Fit calls and sessions | 2 to 4 hours once bookings arrive |

### Sequence

- [ ] **Weeks 1 to 2 (last build window):** record the Doorway Reset, build the opt-in page, write 5 welcome emails, build the fit and safety application form. Use existing skills and Rich's platform.
- [ ] **Weeks 3 to 12:** 4 faceless videos a week on Facebook and Instagram, about $100/month in Meta ads to the Doorway Reset
- [ ] **Workshop #1:** week 5
- [ ] **Workshop #2:** week 9
- [ ] **Workshop #3:** week 12
- [ ] **Day 90 check:** 3 paid Reset Sessions? If yes, raise prices and open the package. If no, find which number fell short (registrants, show-up, or bookings) and fix that one.

### Targets (benchmark guesses, replace after workshop #1)

- 3 Reset Sessions sold
- About 30 workshop attendees across three workshops, assuming 10% book a session
- About 60 to 75 registrants, assuming 40 to 60% of free registrants don't show
