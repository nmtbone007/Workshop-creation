## Formulas

**Revenue per registrant**
`workshop price + (bump price × bump take rate) + (offer price × show-up rate × close rate)`

**Maximum ad spend per registrant** ([DIY Genius](https://www.diygenius.com/the-workshop-funnel/))
Spend no more than revenue per registrant. Aim for the workshop ticket plus bump to cover ad cost, so back-end sales become profit. ([Win Massive Impact](https://winmassiveimpact.com/resources/marketing/low-ticket-sales-funnel))

**Worked example** (placeholder numbers)
- 100 buyers at $47 = $4,700
- 30% take a $27 bump = $810
- 80% show up = 80 attendees
- 8% of attendees buy a $2,000 offer = 6 clients = $12,000
- Total: $17,510
