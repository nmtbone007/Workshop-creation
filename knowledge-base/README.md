## How to read the evidence

Most material here comes from the experts' own podcasts, sales pages, and books, or from third-party summaries. Nobody audits these numbers. Treat every figure as a starting assumption and replace it with your own data after the first workshop.

Each claim carries a tag:

- **[Primary]** The expert's own site, book, or podcast.
- **[Secondary]** A summary, review, or interview written by someone else.
- **[Self-reported]** A result the expert or a student claims without outside verification.
