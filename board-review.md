# Board Review: 90-Day Focus Plan

**Date:** November 10, 2026 · **Reviewed:** `workshop-plan.md` focus plan and `scripts/doorway-reset.md`
**Board:** the advisor lenses named in the board-of-advisors skill (Skeptic, First Principles, Growth, Customer, Operator, Numbers, Long View)

## Verdict: approve with five conditions

The board backs the shape of the plan. It fits the owner's strengths, avoids sales calls, and protects the pen name. The board does not back the traffic math as written. On the current assumptions, the plan likely ends day 90 with zero or one paid session, and the owner would read that as a failed idea instead of an underfed funnel.

---

## 1. The Skeptic

**Concern:** two assumptions carry the whole plan, and nobody has tested either.
- Men who snap will click an ad for a calming audio. Many men in this niche don't see themselves as having a problem, and the word "meditation" can push them away.
- 10% of free workshop attendees will buy a $300 session. Cold-traffic benchmarks in the knowledge base sit at 1 to 3% for free lead to sale.

**Ask:** treat the first 30 days as a test of those two assumptions, not as a launch.

## 2. First Principles

**Concern:** the goal is 3 paid sessions. The plan routes every buyer through a monthly workshop, so a man who is ready in week 2 waits until week 5.

**Ask:** invite readers to apply for a Reset Session in welcome emails 3 and 5. Keep the workshop as a second path.

## 3. The Growth Lens

**Concern:** $100 a month works out to about $3.30 a day. That is below what Meta needs to learn who to show the ad to.

Rough path, with every rate a guess to replace:

| Step | Low | High |
|---|---|---|
| Leads over 10 weeks | 40 | 75 |
| Workshop registrants (about 30%) | 12 | 22 |
| Live attendees (about 30% of cold registrants) | 4 | 7 |
| Sessions at 10% | 0.4 | 0.7 |

**Ask:** keep the $100 monthly total but spend it in bursts of $7 to $10 a day for the 10 to 14 days before each workshop. Add the email invitation from First Principles so leads can buy without attending.

## 4. The Customer

**Concern:** the copy speaks in a coach's words ("hold it in," "reset"). The man's own words are closer to "I come home and bite everyone's head off" or "my wife says I'm always angry."

**Ask:**
- Run the first $10 to $15 ad test on 4 opening lines written in his words, and let cost per lead pick the winner.
- Note for later: partners often push men toward help, so a second ad set aimed at partners may outperform. Test it after day 30.

## 5. The Operator

**Concern:** the weeks 1 to 2 build list leaves out what a buyer touches after saying yes.

**Add to the build list:**
- Payment link for $300 and $500
- Scheduler for the 15-minute fit call
- "Not therapy" disclaimer on the opt-in page and application
- Screening questions plus a referral list for men who need a licensed clinician
- Meta ad account and pixel set up
- A check of what name shows on payment receipts and card statements, since the pen name must hold

**Ask:** write a definition of done for the build window. When those items exist, building stops, including improvements to tools that already work.

## 6. The Numbers

**Concern:** day 90 is the only checkpoint. If the funnel is broken in week 3, the plan finds out in week 12.

**Ask:** add leading indicators.

| Checkpoint | Target |
|---|---|
| Day 21 | Cost per lead known, under $8 |
| Day 45 | 50+ leads, one workshop held, at least 1 application |
| Day 90 | 3 paid Reset Sessions |

If day 45 misses badly, change one variable (opening line, offer wording, or ad budget) instead of starting over.

## 7. The Long View

**Concern:** the plan is sound for 90 days, but two long-term risks start now.
- **Safety and liability.** Hypnotherapy isn't licensed in Georgia, and this niche overlaps with domestic conflict. Screening, the disclaimer, and liability insurance protect the owner before the first paid client, not after.
- **Faceless ceiling.** Faceless content can get a first click, but men buy a $2,000 program from a person. Plan an on-camera welcome video under the pen name by the time the program opens.

---

## Conditions for approval

1. Session invitation in welcome emails 3 and 5
2. Ad spend in pre-workshop bursts, same $100 monthly total
3. First ad test on 4 opening lines in the man's own words
4. Operator items added to the build list, with a written definition of done
5. Day 21 and day 45 checkpoints added

## Dissent

The Skeptic would cut the workshop until the email path proves itself. The rest of the board kept it because the owner enjoys teaching live, and enjoyment keeps a part-time plan alive.
