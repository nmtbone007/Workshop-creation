# Organic List Growth Plan

**Goal:** grow the Doorway Reset email list from Facebook and Instagram with faceless content, at about 2 to 2.5 hours a week of your time.
**Works with:** `workshop-plan.md` (the funnel) and `board-review.md` (the conditions).

---

## How a viewer becomes a subscriber

**Reel or carousel → comment a keyword or tap the bio link → Doorway Reset opt-in page → email list**

Every post asks for one thing: get the free audio. Nothing in the feed sells sessions. The emails do that.

**The comment keyword:** each post ends with "Comment RESET and I'll send you the 10-minute audio." A DM tool sends the opt-in link automatically.
- Commenting takes less effort than finding a bio link, so more people do it.
- Comments also tell Instagram the post is worth showing to more people.
- Tool: ManyChat's free plan covers this. Set it up inside the build window, then leave it alone.

## Content pillars

Rotate these 4 so every week covers each angle once.

| Pillar | What it shows | Example hook (his words) |
|---|---|---|
| The doorway moment | The scene he recognizes | "You were fine in the car. Then you walked in and someone asked you one question." |
| Why it happens | Plain explanation, no medical claims | "You didn't snap at your kid. You snapped at the whole day." |
| 60-second resets | One small tool he can use tonight | "Hand on the door handle. One breath out. Try it tonight." |
| Repair | What to do after he's already snapped | "You snapped. Here's the 20 words that fix most of it." |

**Rules for every post**
- The first line uses his words, not coaching language.
- One idea per post.
- End with the RESET keyword call to action.
- No promises about anger, health, or therapy outcomes.

## Realistic expectations

Organic growth from zero followers is slow and uneven. Most early Reels reach a few hundred people. Occasionally one takes off and brings in a week's worth of subscribers in a day.

| Checkpoint | Organic target (guess) | If missed |
|---|---|---|
| Week 4 | 10+ subscribers, at least 1 Reel over 1,000 views | Change the hooks, not the topic |
| Week 8 | 40+ subscribers | Put the ad bursts behind the 2 best-performing Reels |

**How organic and paid fit together:** organic posting finds the winning hooks for free. The ad money then promotes posts that have already proven themselves instead of guesses. That makes every ad dollar work harder.
